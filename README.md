This is the airline satisfaction playground competition on Kaggle. Just messin' around really, so I'm not gonna write up a super formal rundown on how I did things. It's a pretty fixed process:
I started with XGBoost, tuned that with Optuna. Tried some feature engineering but that didn't really work (or at least, the features I came up with, like total delay time and stuff, were already inferred by the model)
Then, just blended tonnes and tonnes of models together. First ones I tried were LightGBM and CatBoost, which worked out okay, and gave me enough marginal gains to move a bit up the leaderboard. Tuned them with Optuna on a single fold (oh yeah, I'm using 15-fold models, more than the standard 5 just because), before testing the blend.
I was reading up on some transformer stuff, and thought maybe TabPFN could be a nice blend? It's a very different model so should give me a marked boost. Ran it by Claude, Claude said to use TabM instead. Ran experiments for both; a blend with TabPFN performed signnificantly better than blends with both TabPFN and TabM, and blends with just TabM on its own.
Which really just goes to show you what Claude knows.
Currently working on tuning TabM (again with Optuna) in case that does make it viable, but it's looking like a four model blend rn (XGBoost + LightGBM + CatBoost + TabPFN).
Oh yeah, I probably should say; there's a lot of work on tweaking hyperparameters. And in fairness to the TabM suggestion, a low learning rate has really screwed it over, particularly given the early stopping. Looking to tune now without the early stopping, so fingers crossed there.
